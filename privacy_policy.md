# Privacy Policy for Brainstreak

**Last updated: 29 September 2026**

This policy explains how the Brainstreak app for Android ("the app"), published by DasibhaDL ("we", "us"), handles information. The app has no user accounts, and we do not run any servers that receive your data.

## What stays on your device

The following are stored only on your device and are never sent to us:

- **Your training progress.** Game results, levels, skill ratings, Brain Score history, streaks, streak freezes and awards.
- **Your settings.** Theme, sound, haptics and reminder choices.

You can delete this information at any time: use **Settings → Reset progress** in the app, clear the app's storage (Android Settings → Apps → Brainstreak → Storage → Clear data), or uninstall the app.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this data in your device backup so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by us.

## Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a notification on your device at the time you choose. Reminders are created and shown entirely on your device. You can turn them off in the app's settings or in Android's notification settings.

## Sharing

When you share your progress, the app creates an image and text and hands them to the app you pick in Android's share menu. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Ads are never shown during a game. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

- your device's advertising ID
- approximate location, derived from your IP address
- information about the app and your device, such as the device model, operating system and app version
- ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped
- crash logs and diagnostic data from the ads SDK

This information is collected and processed by Google, not by us, and is sent over an encrypted connection. Google's use of it is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and in [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

**Optional rewarded videos.** You can choose to watch a short video to earn a streak freeze. Watching is always optional.

**Your choices:**

- **Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland** are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time under **Settings → Privacy options** in the app.
- **Everyone** can reset or delete their advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your phone).

## Google Play services

The app uses Google Play's in-app review and in-app update features to let you rate the app and to install new versions. These are provided by Google Play under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy); we do not receive any information from them.

## Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Not a medical product

Brainstreak is designed for fun and mental practice. It is not a medical product and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.
